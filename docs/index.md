# Research documentation

LDO studies causal visual loop proposals and the quality of their reported
correspondences. Detection, operational verification, and independent reference
assessment have separate interfaces and separate scientific meanings.

`archive/` preserves the earlier `.docs/` research record byte-for-byte. Its
historical commands and relative links describe earlier layouts; use current
guides above for execution. The [migration ledger](decisions/migration-ledger.json)
maps original paths to their current locations and records their original hashes.

Source-local task READMEs document specialist workflows. They complement the
public mathematical method; they do not replace a study's original protocol.
